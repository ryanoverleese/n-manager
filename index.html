Science Basis & Sources
N Demand: Yield goal × N factor (1.0–1.10 lb N/bu based on yield target). Modern high-yielding hybrids (>240 bu/ac) require ~10% more N per bushel (Haegele et al., 2013; Below et al.).

N Uptake Curve: Bender, Haegele, Ruffo & Below (2013). Agronomy Journal 105:161–170. Cumulative uptake by growth stage: VE-V3=5%, V4-V6=10%, V7-V9=20%, V10-V12=35%, V13-V15=55%, V16-VT=70%, R1=80%, R2=88%, R3=94%, R4=98%, R5-R6=100%.

OM Mineralization: Sandy: 14 lb N/% OM; Medium: 20 lb N/% OM; Fine: 26 lb N/% OM. USDA-NRCS / UNL EC117 / ISU PM2015.

Leaching Losses: Loss per heavy rain event by soil texture: Sandy ~18%, Medium ~10%, Fine ~5%. ISU Extension; Crops.extension.iastate.edu; UNL CropWatch 2022.

Denitrification: Saturation >48 hr: Sandy +8%, Medium +15%, Fine +22%. UNL CropWatch; Bayer Crop Science field research; ISU Integrated Crop Management.

MRTN: Sawyer, Nafziger, Randall, Bundy, Rehm & Joern (2006). Iowa State University PM2015. Current MRTN ranges: C/Sb $4.50/bu = 130–165 lb N/ac, C/C = 165–200 lb N/ac.

Application Timing Efficiency: Fall anhydrous 92%; Spring pre-plant 97%; Sidedress & fertigation 100%. UNL EC117; Melvin & Schepers (1995).
