Science Basis & Sources
N Demand: Yield goal × N factor (1.0–1.10 lb N/bu based on yield target). Modern high-yielding hybrids (>240 bu/ac) require ~10% more N per bushel (Haegele et al., 2013; Below et al.).

N Uptake Curve: Bender, Haegele, Ruffo & Below (2013). Agronomy Journal 105:161–170. Cumulative uptake by growth stage: VE-V3=5%, V4-V6=10%, V7-V9=20%, V10-V12=35%, V13-V15=55%, V16-VT=70%, R1=80%, R2=88%, R3=94%, R4=98%, R5-R6=100%.

OM Mineralization: Sandy: 18 lb N/% OM; Medium: 22 lb N/% OM; Fine: 28 lb N/% OM. UNL EC117 2023, Penn State Extension, Purdue AY-318. Values represent growing-season mineralization (May–Sept) at normal moisture. Sandy soils raised from 14 to 18 to reflect literature floor of ~18–20 lb/% OM per UNL CropWatch.

Leaching Losses: Per qualifying drainage event (>1" rain on near-saturated profile) by texture: Sandy ~18%, Medium ~10%, Fine ~5%. ISU ICM — Nitrogen Losses After Heavy Rains; UNL CropWatch 2022.

Denitrification: Saturation >48 hr: Sandy +3%, Medium +15%, Fine +22%. Sandy reduced from 8% — sandy soils drain in <24 hr and rarely sustain anaerobic conditions (ISU ICM 2014). Medium/Fine rates: 4–5% per day at >65°F × ~3–5 days (ISU Estimating N Losses 2011).

Previous Crop Credits: Soybean: 45 lb/ac non-sandy, 25 lb/ac sandy (UNL EC117 2023). Alfalfa 1st-yr: 80 lb/ac (middle of 50–150 lb range). Legume cover crop (hairy vetch): 50 lb/ac (midpoint of 40–70 lb, UNL CropWatch 2022). Credits per UNL EC117 2023; ISU PM-1501.

Residual Soil Nitrate: Credit all NO₃-N measured in 0–24" profile. UNL EC117 2023 — most significant credit for over-applied or manured fields.

Irrigation Water N: Credit = ppm NO₃-N × inches × 0.227. UNL CropWatch 2023 — "Don't Lose Out on Free Nitrogen from Irrigation Water." NE Platte Valley avg 10–20 ppm; some fields 25+ ppm.

MRTN: Sawyer, Nafziger, Randall, Bundy, Rehm & Joern (2006). Iowa State University PM2015. Current MRTN ranges: C/Sb $4.50/bu = 130–165 lb N/ac, C/C = 165–200 lb N/ac.

Application Timing Efficiency: Fall anhydrous 92%; Spring pre-plant (injected) 97%; Sidedress & fertigation 100%. UNL EC117; Melvin & Schepers (1995).
