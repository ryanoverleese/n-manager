VT/R1 Outlook

Applied N — exactly what you entered for each application.

Mineralization — OM% × 20 lb/ac, a static seasonal estimate of N released from soil organic matter decomposition. (Hergert, UNL EC117)

Previous crop credit — 25 lb/ac for soybean, reflecting residual N from nodule breakdown. (UNL EC117, NRCS)

Leaching & Denitrification — Model-driven from actual season precipitation (Open-Meteo). Leaching triggers when single events exceed the soil texture threshold (0.75"–2.5"). Denitrification activates after 3+ saturated days.

14-day forecast — Upcoming rain bends the prediction using the same leaching model.

VT minimum — 43% of total seasonal N demand must be available at VT/R1 to complete grain fill. (Ciampitti & Vyn 2013)

10/25/50-year range — Open-Meteo historical daily precip, 1973–present, run through the leaching/denitrification model using your soil texture.
